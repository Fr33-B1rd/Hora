Data and services

Daylight runs on your computer. This version uses English and preserves Swedish place names.

Model and maps

Sending a request shares your current message, up to 12 recent messages, current budget, location, time and travel preferences, and a summary of the plan with Nebius. Tool results can include public place names, addresses, opening information, routes, calendar busy intervals and candidate plans. Private calendar titles, descriptions, participants and private locations are not sent to the model. Action records contain tools, arguments and result summaries, not internal reasoning.

Place searches, selected place IDs and travel times are sent to Google Maps. The embedded map connects directly to Google using a separate browser key restricted to allowed origins; server credentials are not sent to the page. Routes are for short-lived display, not persistent storage. Place results remain in the current page and short-lived service session, except for diagnostics below. Place IDs can be used in calendar arrangements you confirm.

Browser sessions retain inputs, choices and confirmation summaries. The local database keeps minimal write previews valid for 15 minutes, conflict snapshots, and the most recently confirmed request and event links for recovery after refresh or restart. Conversation place names can remain in this tab's session storage. Activity verification records (date, place ID, price, session or booking status, source note and timestamp) stay locally for recovery and replanning. Source notes are not sent to the model or Calendar; calendar entries contain activity name, verification status, amount and record time.

Recovery and diagnostics

Requests, retrieved results and progress are held in local service memory for stop, continue and refresh recovery. Recovery lasts up to 15 minutes after completion, can end earlier for capacity, and is lost on restart. Expired records are cleared on subsequent access. Continuing rechecks calendar and itinerary constraints.

Local diagnostics record model messages and tool definitions, visible tool arguments, state changes, checks, errors and output usage. They exclude authentication headers, service keys, internal reasoning and private calendar details. Logs may contain original messages, public places and busy intervals; they are not separately uploaded. Files in data/diagnostics are limited to 20 files of 4 MB each. Files older than 24 hours are cleaned when the next diagnostic starts. Set DAYLIGHT_DIAGNOSTICS=0 to stop future recording; existing files can be deleted locally.

Voice and conversation

The microphone starts only with Start speaking. Recordings last at most 60 seconds and stop when you leave the planning page or switch to the background. Browser MediaRecorder audio goes only to Daylight at 127.0.0.1 and is transcribed in memory by local faster-whisper. Audio is not forwarded to cloud recognition or saved in audio files. Cancel aborts the request. Initial setup downloads the model; runtime loading is offline.

Unsent transcripts remain in page memory. Up to 20 messages, current preferences and source cards are saved in this tab's sessionStorage and restored on refresh. New conversation clears the current conversation, not calendar arrangements. This English version starts a separate context; earlier-language browser records remain untouched. Sending text follows the model rules above. Read-aloud prefers an English device voice; other voices may use the browser's service.

News and activities

News uses public SVT Stockholm RSS titles and excerpts from the last 72 hours. Events use curated Visit Stockholm listings, filtered by straight-line distance from a stated landmark. Titles and contextual excerpts may be sent to Nebius for English headlines, preserving original names. Translation does not prove the full article was read or verified. Cards retain original titles, links and dates. Failed queries and empty results are disclosed.

Calendar and credentials

Google Calendar authorization allows reading and managing events in calendars you own. This version accesses only the primary calendar, reads the selected day for conflicts, and creates or updates Daylight's own events only after you confirm a preview. It does not send invitations, delete other events, pay, buy tickets or book merchants.

Tokens are encrypted in the local data folder. The key is in the same folder, so someone who can read the entire folder can decrypt them. Use a protected system account and do not share that folder. Disconnect removes local tokens and write previews, not already-created events. You can also revoke authorization in your Google account's third-party access settings.

Opening hours do not establish appointment availability. Prices and user input may change. Shanghai pages are a separate fictional demo.

Map use is subject to Google Maps Terms and Google Privacy Policy. This prototype is for personal local use; public deployment needs appropriate privacy terms, account security and quota controls.
